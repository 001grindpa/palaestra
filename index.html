I

Open Match Book

A creator writes a dated question, picks a YES or NO position, sets event and resolve dates, attaches two independent sources, and posts the stake.

II

Matched Stake

A counterparty joins by matching the creator's exact stake. The contest transitions to MATCHED and locks reserved stakes until the resolve date.

III

Dual-Source Settlement

On or after resolve_after, validators examine both source pages. If both agree, the winner receives the pot. If inconclusive, timeout refunds open the next day.
